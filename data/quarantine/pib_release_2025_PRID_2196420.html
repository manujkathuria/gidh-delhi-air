Prime Minister chairs DGP/IGP Conference in Raipur

Posted On: 29 NOV 2025 8:24PM by PIB Delhi

Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi chaired the first day of the Conference of Directors General and Inspectors General of Police in Raipur today. He remarked that the conference witnessed extensive deliberations on different aspects of India’s security system. Prime Minister underscored that this forum serves as a vital platform to share best practices and innovations in the field of national security.

In a post on X, Shri Modi said:

“The first day of the DGP/IGP Conference in Raipur witnessed extensive deliberations on different aspects of India’s security system. This is a great forum to share best practices and innovations in this field.”
