# Episode 001 — Can You Find 30 Clean Days in Delhi?

Status: concept approved; analysis pending. Python investigation ID: `clean-days`. Tracking: https://github.com/manujkathuria/gidh-delhi-air/issues/3

## Viewer question

Could we find one month's worth of days with low fine-particle pollution in Delhi during 2025?

## Story device

A calendar, separate Indian and WHO benchmark counters, and three escalating questions: can one station reach 30 qualifying days; do the same days qualify at the other monitored locations; can we find 30 consecutive qualifying days?

## Research decisions to settle

- Define the daily PM2.5 completeness requirement before counting.
- Record benchmark values, averaging periods and source citations explicitly.
- Define how a multi-station day qualifies and how missing stations affect the count.
- Mark days with insufficient observations as unknown, never clean or zero.
- Examine PM10 as an additional check; PM2.5 alone does not establish overall clean air.

## Publication status

No counts, streak lengths or conclusions are available yet. The working title uses accessible language; the video and website must explain what actually qualifies. Scope is seven monitored locations, not every resident's exposure. Diwali 2026 is outside the available telemetry period.

## Planned outputs

A daily calendar export, station comparisons, a reproducible methodology page, downloadable aggregate data, and video charts built from the same results.
