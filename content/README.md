# Video series and editorial work

North star: **Turn a question we care about into a story people want to finish watching.**

Each episode gets a numbered directory containing its brief, then its evidence-backed findings, script, storyboard and production notes as they are developed. Use findings from the research pipeline; keep untested ideas distinct from publishable claims.

Large video, audio and editing-project files belong in external production storage. This repository holds text, code, and small reproducible research/chart assets.
